### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a multidisciplinary software engineer and systems researcher focused on **low-level computer architecture**, **bare-metal firmware**, **high-performance digital signal processing (DSP)**, and **production ML observability**. 

- 🚀 **Core Focus**: Eliminating virtualization latency in modern browsers through targeted micro-interception architectures and hardware-level instruction traps.
- 📜 **Research**: Author of ***Targeted Bus-Cycle Interception: A Low-Latency WebAssembly Bridge for 16-Bit Bare-Metal Audio Synthesis and Multi-Timbral Acoustic Resynthesis*** (Camera-Ready IEEE Conference Publication).
- 🔬 **Engineering Philosophy**: Writing close-to-the-metal code, measuring nanosecond-level empirical telemetry, and crafting rich, responsive user interfaces.
